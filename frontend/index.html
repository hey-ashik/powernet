The Problem

Hidden Power Inefficiencies Cost Real Revenue

Traditional electrical installations operate in the dark, leading to equipment stress, unmonitored tariff penalties, and unplanned breaker outages.

Unmonitored Peak Spikes

Surpassing contracted breaker limits triggers unexpected shutdowns and costly utility peak demand surcharges.

Low Power Factor Losses

Uncompensated inductive machinery degrades system power factor, drawing excess current and wearing out conductors.

Delayed Energy Audits

Relying on monthly bills means finding out about equipment faults and phantom consumption weeks after damage occurred.

The Solution

Sub-Second Telemetry Coupled with AI Predictive Control

PowerNet bridges hardware sensors with instant cloud telemetry to give engineers continuous visibility and automated load forecasting.

Automated IoT Telemetry

Sensors stream voltage, current, and active wattage at sub-second frequencies over lightweight MQTT broker queues.

AI Peak Breaker Forecast

Predictive algorithms analyze load curves to project breaker overloads in advance, preventing breaker tripping.

Instant Device Pairing

Simple Device ID binding (e.g. pnw101) links equipment instantly to your account with zero firmware friction.

System Architecture

Built for Industrial Uptime & Resilience

Engineered with edge-to-cloud redundancy, TLS-encrypted telemetry, and zero-dependency microservices.

HiveMQ MQTT Cloud Broker

Bi-directional telemetry streaming over standard port 1883 with automatic reconnection, heartbeat keepalive, and payload verification.

1,000ms heartbeat • Zero socket starvation buffer

Dual-Ingestion Fallback

Full resilience for shared hosting environments. If MQTT CLI daemon access is unavailable, telemetry automatically falls back to direct REST endpoints.

HTTPS REST fallback • Hostinger Apache / Nginx ready

Hardware Telemetry Stack

Compatible with PZEM-004T and ACS712 current transformers. Pre-configured firmware computes true RMS current, active power, and cumulative kWh.

True RMS computation • Built-in flash memory safety

AI Forecast & Peak Shaving

Real-time telemetry feeds regression models that forecast hourly energy trends, anomalous power spikes, and scheduled load shifts.

Historical energy analytics • Breaker protection limits
